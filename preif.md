# Mobil Distributor Egypt Landing Page Brief

**Working project label:** Mobility  
**Market:** Egypt  
**Page type:** B2B lead-generation landing page  
**Primary outcome:** Qualified inquiries for Mobil industrial and fleet lubricants, technical services, and supply support.

## 1. Project scope

Build a credible landing page for an authorized Mobil distributor serving industrial and fleet customers in Egypt. The page should position the business as more than a lubricant supplier: it combines Mobil product access, local stock and logistics, and engineering support that helps customers choose and manage lubricants across their equipment.

The attached layout is the primary source for the proposed page content. The earlier Mobil Service KSA branding review is a separate consumer vehicle-maintenance brand. Use it only as a broad reference for Mobil's blue-led visual character. Do not reuse its Saudi service-center claims, app journey, vehicle-service packages, phone number, hours, or KSA imagery for this Egypt distributor page.

“Mobility” is used as the working project name because it appears in the supplied layout. Confirm the distributor's legal and trading name before publishing.

## 2. Business context

- **Business:** Mobil distributor in Egypt; the business context identifies Petro Group as a Mobil distributor.
- **Existing commercial footprint:** Customers in 10th of Ramadan and Obour.
- **Growth objective:** Reach industrial zones and free zones across Egypt, with marine customers also in scope.
- **Priority sectors:** Food manufacturing, cement, hydraulic industries, and other industrial users; marine and fleet accounts are expansion audiences.
- **Buying roles:** Maintenance and lubrication engineers, procurement teams, plant managers, and fleet decision-makers.
- **Desired customer action:** Request a quote, technical consultation, site survey, or product recommendation.

## 3. Positioning and value proposition

### Positioning statement

For industrial operators and fleet owners in Egypt who need reliable lubricant supply and better equipment performance, Mobility provides Mobil lubricants supported by local logistics and practical lubrication engineering. Customers can get product guidance, on-site support, oil analysis, and maintenance programs through one distributor relationship.

### Core promise

**More than just oils — total engineering solutions.**

### Customer value

- Access to Mobil industrial and commercial-vehicle lubricant portfolios.
- Local supply capability supported by Egyptian logistics hubs.
- Technical help matching lubricants to equipment and operating conditions.
- Condition monitoring and oil-management services that can support preventive maintenance and cost control.

Keep the wording focused on support and potential operational benefits. Do not promise a specific reduction in downtime, lubricant consumption, cost, or failure rate without documented evidence and approved wording.

## 4. Audience priorities

| Audience | Main concern | Relevant page response |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance / lubrication engineers | Correct product selection, contamination, wear, machine reliability | Mobil Serv Oil Analysis, site surveys, filtration, product recommendations |
| Procurement | Availability, authenticity, delivery, price and payment terms | Authorized-distributor proof, local stock, logistics coverage, quote request |
| Plant managers | Production continuity, maintenance planning, total operating cost | Technical support, predictive-maintenance programs, TCO optimization |
| Fleet managers | Vehicle uptime, dependable supply, commercial-vehicle lubricant choice | Mobil Delvac portfolio, fleet support, delivery and account inquiry |
| Marine and free-zone operators | Supply access and technical fit in their operating location | Confirmed coverage map, contact route, product and service consultation |

## 5. Message hierarchy

1. **Lead:** Authorized Mobil distribution in Egypt, with engineering support beyond product supply.
2. **Product range:** Industrial and commercial-vehicle lubricants for defined applications.
3. **Technical capability:** Oil analysis, field engineering, site surveys, filtration, and lubricant optimization.
4. **Supply capability:** Egyptian stock and logistics hubs, with geographic coverage stated precisely.
5. **Conversion:** Request a quote or technical consultation through a short lead form.

## 6. Page structure and content direction

### Section 1 — Hero

**Headline from the supplied layout:**  
“Your Authorized Mobil Distributor in Egypt”

**Supporting headline:**  
“More than just oils — we provide total engineering solutions.”

**Subheadline from the supplied layout:**  
“As a trusted official Mobil distributor, we deliver authentic industrial lubrication, local warehousing, and expert technical support to help keep your operations running.”

**Primary CTA recommendation:** `Request a Technical Consultation`  
**Secondary CTA recommendation:** `Request a Quote`

The supplied layout also proposes optional trust badges: “Official ExxonMobil Authorized Distributor” and “Certified Authenticity & Factory Direct Assurance.” Use these only after authorization and sourcing language are approved and the corresponding proof is available.

### Section 2 — Why Mobility

Present four concise proof-led cards:

1. **Local storage and product integrity** — The layout claims dedicated storage inside ExxonMobil facilities and says this ensures product integrity. Verify the exact storage arrangement and approved wording before publishing.
2. **Alexandria logistics advantage** — The layout names Amreya and Borg El Arab and describes their proximity to the port. Confirm the facilities, location details, and current logistics benefit.
3. **Flexible commercial support** — The layout proposes competitive pricing, flexible payment terms, and invoices in foreign currencies. Confirm which terms and currencies are actually available and any eligibility conditions.
4. **Stock and dispatch capability** — The layout lists hubs in 10th of Ramadan, Borg El Arab, Amreya, and Obour. Confirm operational status, stock depth, delivery scope, and whether any delivery time is guaranteed.

### Section 3 — Product portfolios

**Industrial lubricants**

- Mobil DTE 20 Series
- Mobilgear 600 XP Series

**Commercial and fleet lubricants**

- Mobil Delvac MX 20W-50
- Mobil Delvac 1350 — “The Legend” appears as a descriptor in the supplied layout; confirm the exact product name and approved descriptor.
- Mobil Delvac Modern 15W-40

Use current product names, technical data, packaging, and application claims from approved ExxonMobil product materials. Do not imply every grade is stocked at every hub.

### Section 4 — Engineering services

1. **Mobil Serv Oil Analysis** — Used-oil testing and interpretation to help assess equipment condition and identify maintenance concerns. The layout describes predictive-maintenance value; validate all technical and performance claims with the service owner.
2. **On-site Field Engineering Services and site surveys** — Scheduled visits by qualified lubrication engineers / DLEs to review equipment applications and recommend suitable lubricants. Confirm the DLE title, credentials, visit availability, and whether visits are scheduled or on request.
3. **Total Cost of Ownership optimization** — Tailored lubricant-management programs intended to support longer oil life, controlled consumption, and operating efficiency. Present outcomes as goals or case-backed results, not guarantees.
4. **Industrial oil filtration** — On-site filtration intended to remove contaminants and support continued oil use where technically suitable. Confirm service scope, equipment types, and limits with the engineering team.

### Section 5 — Lead capture form

Fields in the supplied layout:

- Full name
- Company / factory / fleet name
- Phone number
- Industry sector
- Primary request (optional)

Recommended additions, if the sales team can act on them:

- Work email
- City / operating location
- Product or service of interest
- Preferred contact method

Keep the form short. State how the business will use submitted details and link to the applicable privacy notice. Form destination, CRM routing, consent wording, and response-time commitment are not specified and must be confirmed before launch.

### Section 6 — Logistics and coverage map

The supplied layout proposes a map of operational hubs serving Egypt. It lists these pins:

- Amreya
- Borg El Arab
- 10th of Ramadan
- Amshas

Elsewhere in the same layout, the logistics hubs listed are Amreya, Borg El Arab, 10th of Ramadan, and Obour. Previous business context also identifies Borg El Arab and 10th of Ramadan warehouses and existing customers in 10th of Ramadan and Obour. These lists do not fully match. Confirm the correct locations and the distinction between warehouse, office, customer base, and delivery hub before creating the map or claiming nationwide coverage.

## 7. Design and brand direction

- Use official Mobil / ExxonMobil identity files and current brand standards. The earlier KSA page review found a blue-led Mobil look with red used as a restrained brand accent, but its sampled color values were visual estimates and should not be treated as official specifications.
- Keep this page visually distinct from Mobil Service KSA's consumer maintenance site. This is an Egypt B2B distribution and engineering page, so use industrial settings, product/application imagery, warehouses, field engineers, oil analysis, and fleet or plant environments.
- Do not use KSA vehicle-service center, retail maintenance package, app-booking, or On The Go van content on this page.
- Use strong, clean typography, high contrast, concise content blocks, and a responsive layout.
- Show authentic Mobil product packaging only; do not redraw or alter labels.
- Use approved ExxonMobil, Mobil, customer, and partner logos only. Do not add a logo as a trust badge unless its use is authorized.
- Favor real industrial photography over generic stock images. Avoid visuals that imply a site, stock level, service, or customer relationship that has not been confirmed.

## 8. Conversion and measurement

### Primary conversion

Completed inquiry form from a qualified industrial, fleet, marine, or free-zone prospect.

### Secondary conversions

- Click-to-call or WhatsApp inquiry, once official contact details are supplied.
- Product or service inquiry initiated from the relevant section.
- Request for a site survey or oil-analysis consultation.

### Recommended launch measures

Track form starts and completions, qualified-lead rate, lead source, sector, location, requested service/product, and sales follow-up outcome. Set numeric targets only after baseline traffic and sales capacity are known.

| Foreign-currency invoices | Permitted currencies, legal / finance approval, and customer eligibility |

If a claim cannot be verified, remove it or rewrite it narrowly. In particular, avoid “only,” “guarantee,” “nationwide,” and “immediate” unless the business has approved evidence and clear conditions.
