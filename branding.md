# Mobil Service KSA — Brand Guide

> **Purpose:** A working brand and landing-page reference based on the Arabic homepage at [mobilserviceksa.com/ar](https://mobilserviceksa.com/ar), reviewed on 22 October 2026. It records what the page communicates and how it presents the brand. Color values below are visual estimates from the rendered page, not extracted design tokens; confirm them against approved source files before production use.

## 1. Brand at a glance

- **Brand name:** Mobil Service KSA
- **Arabic name:** موبيل سيرفس
- **Descriptor used in the header:** Mobil Service by APSCO
- **Category:** Vehicle maintenance and service centers in Saudi Arabia
- **Brand promise:** Professional, dependable vehicle care that gives drivers peace of mind.
- **Homepage lead line:** «راحة بالك علينا»
- **Brand idea:** Trusted expertise, genuine products, and convenient maintenance in one service experience.
- **Primary market:** Drivers and vehicle owners in Saudi Arabia. The site also has a dedicated Business Services area for organizations and fleets.
- **Language direction:** Arabic-first, right-to-left experience, with an English language option.

## 2. Brand positioning

**Positioning statement**  
For drivers who want to maintain their vehicles with confidence, Mobil Service KSA provides accessible vehicle-care services through trained technicians, genuine Mobil products, and a network of service centers. It combines technical credibility with clear, convenient service options.

**Customer need**  
“I want my vehicle maintained properly, with trustworthy advice and less uncertainty about where to go or what to book.”

**Value proposition**  
Expert care, genuine products, and a straightforward path from choosing a service to booking it.

**Brand character**

- **Expert:** Certified technicians and advanced service equipment.
- **Trustworthy:** Genuine Mobil 1 products, recommended parts, and practical recommendations.
- **Convenient:** Service centers, an on-the-go van service, a mobile app, and WhatsApp contact.
- **Reassuring:** The experience is framed around peace of mind and confident driving.
- **Established:** The homepage presents a service network across more than 20 Saudi cities and an APSCO association.

## 3. Audience and customer context

### Primary: Individual drivers

Drivers seeking routine maintenance, oil changes, inspections, or a nearby service center. They value clear options, vehicle care from trained technicians, and confidence in the products used.

### Secondary: Convenience-led customers

Drivers who want to locate a center, choose a service, book a visit, or use the Mobil Service On The Go van at their door.

### Business audience

The site navigation includes a Business Services section. Treat fleet operators and organizations as a relevant audience across the wider site, but do not assume the consumer homepage contains specific fleet terms, offers, or service commitments unless those are confirmed on the business page.

## 4. Messaging framework

### Core message

**راحة بالك علينا** — Mobil Service takes care of vehicle maintenance through professional service and trusted products.

### Supporting messages

1. **Professional expertise:** Technicians are trained and certified through ExxonMobil programs.
2. **Genuine products and parts:** The page highlights Mobil 1™ products and parts recommended by vehicle manufacturers.
3. **Convenient access:** Customers can find a center, select a service, book an appointment, and earn rewards through the app.
4. **Broad availability:** The homepage states that service centers operate in more than 20 cities across the Kingdom.
5. **Flexible maintenance:** Care and Care Plus packages offer different inspection scopes; the homepage describes them as 15 and 27 services respectively.
6. **More ways to get service:** The page features Mobil Service On The Go and weekly offers.

### Proof points shown on the homepage

- Certified technicians and advanced equipment.
- Genuine Mobil 1™ products and manufacturer-recommended parts.
- Service centers in more than 20 cities in Saudi Arabia.
- A four-step app journey: find a center, choose a service, book an appointment, earn rewards.
- Care and Care Plus maintenance packages.
- Apple App Store and Google Play links.
- WhatsApp and phone contact at **920003525**.

Keep claims tied to the page or current approved business information. Do not invent service guarantees, repair outcomes, prices, response times, or coverage beyond the stated network.

## 5. Voice and writing style

### Voice attributes

- **Confident, never boastful.** Explain the expertise behind the service without exaggerated superlatives.
- **Reassuring, never alarming.** Help drivers feel informed and supported.
- **Practical and clear.** Use familiar vehicle-care terms and say what the customer can do next.
- **Professional and approachable.** Use polished Arabic that feels natural to Saudi readers without forcing slang.
- **Customer-centered.** Lead with the driver's needs, then explain the service or proof point.

### Arabic language guidance

- Write in clear, contemporary Arabic suited to Saudi Arabia.
- Keep the main site copy in Arabic when the Arabic experience is selected; preserve product and trademark names such as **Mobil 1™** in their approved form.
- Use concise sentences and familiar terms: «احجز موعدك»، «اختر خدمتك»، «مراكز الخدمة».
- Maintain correct RTL layout, punctuation, numerals, and mixed Arabic/Latin brand names.
- Use «موبيل سيرفس» consistently for the Arabic brand name. Keep «Mobil Service» for English contexts and approved logo artwork.
- Prefer clear CTAs such as «تعرّف على خدماتنا»، «اكتشف المزيد»، «تواصل معنا» and «احجز موعدك».

### Copy pattern

1. State the customer benefit.
2. Explain the service or capability in plain language.
3. Add a credible proof point.
4. Offer one clear next action.

**Example:**  
«حافظ على أداء سيارتك مع فنيين معتمدين ومنتجات Mobil 1™ الأصلية. اختر الخدمة المناسبة واحجز موعدك.»

This is a suggested copy pattern, not a quotation from the site.

### Avoid

- Fear-based language about vehicle failure or safety.
- Unsupported claims such as “best in Saudi Arabia,” “guaranteed results,” or “instant service.”
- Dense technical language without a customer benefit.
- Informal slang that weakens the professional service tone.
- Changing trademark spelling, logo lockups, or product names.

## 6. Visual identity

### Color palette

The live page uses a strong Mobil-style blue with white space, black Arabic typography, and light-gray section backgrounds. The following hex values are **approximate visual references**, not confirmed CSS values.

| Role | Approximate value | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Primary service blue | `#164B98` | Main CTA buttons, emphasis, icon strokes, feature panels |
| Deep blue | `#0B2E62` | Darker blue emphasis where needed; keep secondary to primary blue |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Header, cards, and clean content areas |
| Light gray | `#F3F4F5` | Alternating section backgrounds and neutral surfaces |
| Near-black | `#111111` | Main Arabic headings and body text |
| Mobil red accent | `#E31B34` | Restrained brand accent only where present in approved Mobil assets |

The Mobil Service and APSCO marks may contain additional colors. Use the supplied logo artwork for those colors; do not rebuild the marks from the table above. Before creating production assets, sample the current approved logo and site styles to confirm exact color values and contrast.

### Logo and brand architecture

- Use the approved **Mobil Service by APSCO** lockup when the partnership or operator identity is shown.
- Keep the Mobil Service and APSCO marks in their approved proportions, colors, and relationship.
- Do not stretch, redraw, recolor, crop, or add effects to either mark.
- Keep clear space around the lockup and use a high-resolution approved asset on light or dark backgrounds.
- Treat **Mobil 1™** as a product mark. Do not substitute it for the service-network logo.
- Use manufacturer or partner logos only from approved assets and preserve their original colors and proportions.

### Typography

- The page uses a bold Arabic sans-serif style for headlines and a simpler, highly readable Arabic sans-serif for body copy.
- Headings are heavy and high-contrast; body text is regular-weight with comfortable line spacing.
- Use the current website font files or an approved Arabic sans-serif equivalent. The exact font family was not confirmed from the rendered page.
- Maintain clear hierarchy: small blue section label, strong headline, short supporting paragraph, then CTA.
- Avoid overly condensed Arabic type, decorative scripts, or long all-bold paragraphs.

### Photography and imagery

Use imagery that makes service quality and convenience tangible:

- Real technicians working carefully on vehicles, with appropriate workwear and safe workshop practices.
- Genuine Mobil products shown clearly and without altering packaging, labels, or proportions.
- Service-center, vehicle, and mobile-van scenes that feel clean, credible, and Saudi-market appropriate.
- Natural, neutral lighting and realistic vehicle colors.
- Wide hero photography with a dark overlay when white text sits on top; keep the image legible without obscuring the service activity.
- Product and service imagery that supports the section instead of acting as decoration.

Avoid generic luxury-car imagery, unsafe workshop behavior, visibly counterfeit packaging, exaggerated retouching, or imagery that promises a service the business does not provide.

### Iconography and graphics

- Use simple, clean line icons in the brand blue for service and feature explanations.
- Keep icon stroke weight and visual scale consistent across a section.
- Use real product or workshop photography as the primary visual proof; icons should clarify, not replace, it.
- Use small blue dots, lines, or labels as accents, as in the navigation and section labels.

## 7. Landing-page design system

### Layout and hierarchy

- Preserve right-to-left reading order in Arabic.
- Use a clear header with the brand lockup, primary navigation, language switch, and visible contact options.
- Let the hero lead with one benefit-led headline, concise support copy, and one dominant CTA.
- Organize the page into distinct sections: service access, why choose Mobil Service, services, packages, offers, app journey, partners, news, and contact/footer.
- Alternate white and pale-gray surfaces to separate content without visual clutter.
- Use generous spacing and large Arabic headings; keep paragraphs short and easy to scan.
- On small screens, stack cards and columns in a logical RTL order and keep key booking/contact actions easy to reach.

### Components

- **Primary CTA:** Solid blue, pill-shaped button with white text. Use for the most important action in a section.
- **Secondary CTA:** White or transparent button with a blue outline and blue text.
- **Cards:** White surfaces with generous internal spacing and rounded corners. The observed page uses visibly rounded cards; match the current site rather than imposing a new radius system.
- **Feature panel:** Blue surface with white copy and a contrasting white CTA for a major proof point such as center coverage.
- **Service list:** Use concise service labels with consistent icons or image markers.
- **Package comparison:** Make package names and service counts easy to compare; avoid compressing long lists into unreadable cards.
- **Contact affordances:** Keep phone and WhatsApp links recognizable and easy to tap.

## 10. Accessibility and usability

- Maintain strong text contrast on blue and photographic backgrounds.
- Keep Arabic text readable at mobile sizes, with comfortable line height and short line lengths.
- Preserve semantic heading order and meaningful Arabic labels for links and controls.
- Do not communicate service status or package differences by color alone.
- Provide useful alt text for informative images; mark purely decorative imagery appropriately.
- Keep buttons large enough to tap and use clear action labels rather than generic «اضغط هنا».
- Ensure mixed Arabic/English terms, phone numbers, and app links remain legible in RTL layouts.

## 11. Quick do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Lead with peace of mind, expertise, genuine products, or convenience. | Lead with fear or unsupported promises. |
| Use approved Mobil Service, APSCO, and Mobil 1™ assets. | Recreate, recolor, stretch, or combine brand marks yourself. |
| Use clear Arabic and one primary CTA per section. | Overload sections with multiple competing calls to action. |
| Show realistic service work and accurate products. | Alter product labels or imply unavailable services. |
| Preserve the blue, white, and light-neutral visual balance. | Add new colors that compete with the brand blue. |
| Make customer actions clear: find, choose, book, contact. | Use vague CTAs or hide contact details. |
| Verify service counts, hours, coverage, and offers before reuse. | Treat page content as a permanent price list or guarantee. |

## 12. Items to confirm before production use
