# Mobility — Mobil Distributor Egypt Landing Page

B2B lead-generation landing page for an authorized Mobil distributor serving industrial, fleet, and marine customers in Egypt.

**Core promise:** More than just oils — total engineering solutions.

|---|---|

Source documents:

## 1. Business context

- Mobil distributor in Egypt; prior business context identifies **Petro Group** as the distributor.
- Existing customers in **10th of Ramadan** and **Obour**.
- Growth objective: industrial zones and free zones across Egypt, plus marine accounts.
- Priority sectors: food manufacturing, cement, hydraulic industries, and other industrial users. Marine and fleet are expansion audiences.
- Buying roles: maintenance/lubrication engineers, procurement teams, plant managers, fleet decision-makers.
- Desired action: request a quote, technical consultation, site survey, or product recommendation.

> The KSA branding review is a separate consumer vehicle-maintenance brand. Use it only as broad reference for Mobil's blue-led visual character — not for service-center claims, packages, app journeys, contact details, or imagery.

## 2. Positioning

For industrial operators and fleet owners in Egypt who need reliable lubricant supply and better equipment performance, Mobility provides Mobil lubricants supported by local logistics and practical lubrication engineering. Customers can get product guidance, on-site support, oil analysis, and maintenance programs through one distributor relationship.

**Customer value**

- Access to Mobil industrial and commercial-vehicle lubricant portfolios.
- Local supply capability supported by Egyptian logistics hubs.
- Technical help matching lubricants to equipment and operating conditions.
- Condition monitoring and oil-management services supporting preventive maintenance and cost control.

Keep wording focused on support and potential operational benefits. Do not promise a specific reduction in downtime, lubricant consumption, cost, or failure rate without documented evidence and approved wording.

## 3. Message hierarchy

1. **Lead** — Authorized Mobil distribution in Egypt, with engineering support beyond product supply.
2. **Product range** — Industrial and commercial-vehicle lubricants for defined applications.
3. **Technical capability** — Oil analysis, field engineering, site surveys, filtration, lubricant optimization.
4. **Supply capability** — Egyptian stock and logistics hubs, with coverage stated precisely.
5. **Conversion** — Request a quote or technical consultation through a short lead form.

### Product portfolios

**Industrial**

- Mobil DTE 20 Series
- Mobilgear 600 XP Series

**Commercial and fleet**

- Mobil Delvac MX 20W-50
- Mobil Delvac 1350 — “The Legend” descriptor to confirm
- Mobil Delvac Modern 15W-40

Use current product names, technical data, packaging, and application claims from approved ExxonMobil materials. Do not imply every grade is stocked at every hub.

### Engineering services

1. **Mobil Serv Oil Analysis** — used-oil testing and interpretation to assess equipment condition and flag maintenance concerns.
2. **Field Engineering Services and site surveys** — scheduled visits by qualified lubrication engineers to review applications and recommend suitable lubricants.
3. **Total Cost of Ownership optimization** — tailored lubricant-management programs aimed at longer oil life, controlled consumption, and operating efficiency. Present outcomes as goals or case-backed results, not guarantees.
4. **Industrial oil filtration** — on-site filtration to remove contaminants and support continued oil use where technically suitable.

## 5. Brand direction

Blue-led Mobil identity with red used sparingly as an accent. The page must feel visually distinct from the KSA consumer maintenance site: industrial settings, product/application imagery, warehouses, field engineers, oil analysis, and fleet or plant environments.

Approximate visual reference (from [`branding.md`](branding.md) — confirm against approved assets before production):

Guidelines:

- Use official Mobil / ExxonMobil identity files and current brand standards.
- Show authentic Mobil product packaging only; never redraw or alter labels.
- Use approved logos only; do not add a logo as a trust badge without authorization.
- Favor real industrial photography over generic stock. Avoid visuals implying unconfirmed sites, stock, services, or customer relationships.
- Strong, clean typography, high contrast, concise content blocks, responsive layout.

## 6. Claims to verify before publication

| Proposed claim | Verification needed |
|---|---|
| Official / authorized ExxonMobil Mobil distributor | Current authorization, permitted designation, territory, logo usage |
| “100% authentic” / factory-direct assurance | Approved authenticity language and supply-chain evidence |
| 2,000+ industrial and fleet clients | Source, client definition, as-of date |
| 48-hour rapid delivery guarantee | Covered locations, cutoff, stock conditions, exclusions, remedy |
| Nationwide coverage across Egypt | Actual delivery map and limits by product/location |
| Dedicated warehouses inside ExxonMobil facilities / “only distributor” | Written evidence and approval for exclusivity |
| Alexandria facilities closest to the port | Correct addresses and substantiation |
| Guaranteed inventory / immediate stock | Current stock policy and availability language |
| Competitive pricing and flexible payment | Eligibility, credit approval, approved terms |
| Foreign-currency invoices | Permitted currencies and finance/legal approval |
| DLE credentials and scheduled site surveys | Current roster, qualifications, availability |
| Product availability and “The Legend” descriptor | Current product list, grade names, packaging, trademark approval |

If a claim cannot be verified, remove it or rewrite it narrowly. Avoid **“only,” “guarantee,” “nationwide,”** and **“immediate”** unless the business has approved evidence and clear conditions.

## 8. Running the site

The app lives in [`v1/`](v1/) and uses Next.js 16 with React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, and TypeScript.

```bash
cd v1
npm install
npm run dev      # http://localhost:3000
npm run build    # production build
npm run lint
```
